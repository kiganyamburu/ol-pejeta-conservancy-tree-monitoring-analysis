# Ol Pejeta Conservancy Tree Monitoring Report

**Period:** 2023 to 2025  
**Data sources:** Tagged trees and seedling density workbook, plus rainfall series supplied by the client

## Purpose

Assess tree survival, growth, damage patterns, density change, ant associations, and the possible influence of rainfall in Ol Pejeta Conservancy.

## Methods Used

- Tagged-tree outcomes were summarized for survival, mortality, growth, height classes, damagers, and zone comparison.
- Density records were compared between 2023 and 2025.
- Drought was treated as a damager category.
- Missing trees were excluded from mortality calculations because missing is not the same as dead.

## Key Results

| Measure | Result |
|---|---:|
| Tagged trees with 2023 height | 1,949 |
| Alive in 2025 | 1,719 |
| Dead in 2025 | 167 |
| Missing in 2025 | 49 |
| Mortality excluding missing | 8.79% |
| Mean height change in alive trees | +0.09 cm |
| Growth test | Not significant |

## Zone Comparison

| Zone | Mean growth | Mortality |
|---|---:|---:|
| Excluded plots | +14.28 cm | 1.01% |
| Non-excluded plots | -0.77 cm | 9.22% |

Excluded plots performed substantially better than non-excluded plots in both growth and survival.

## Main Damagers

- Elephant was the dominant damager.
- Rhino was the next major damager.
- Giraffe and other browsers also contributed.
- Drought/natural drying was recorded but was not the main driver.

Damage height followed the expected pattern:

- Rhino damage was concentrated lower on the tree.
- Giraffe damage was concentrated higher on the tree.
- Elephant damage occurred across a broad height range.

## Density Findings

| Year | Mean density per ha | Mean height | Mean length |
|---|---:|---:|---:|
| 2023 | 2,573.2 | 39.73 cm | 49.67 cm |
| 2025 | 2,292.2 | 53.39 cm | 67.21 cm |

- Density declined significantly from 2023 to 2025.
- Height and length were strongly related.

## Ant Findings

- Crematogaster occupancy increased from 13.60% in 2023 to 16.21% in 2025.
- Ant presence was associated with damage status in the density data.

## Rainfall Context

| Year | Annual rainfall |
|---|---:|
| 2023 | 839.4 mm |
| 2024 | 1,120.8 mm |
| 2025 | 1,040.4 mm |

Rainfall was highest in 2024 and remained relatively high in 2025, which helps contextualize tree performance and drought-related damage.

## Conclusion

Tree survival and growth were much better in excluded plots, elephant and rhino were the main sources of damage, density declined over time, and missing trees should not be counted as dead in mortality estimates.
